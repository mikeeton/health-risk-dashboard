# Structured usability evaluation protocol

No participant results are pre-populated or manufactured. Obtain the required
ethics, consent, privacy, and organisational approvals before recruiting people
or recording results.

## Evaluation stages

1. Two reviewers independently complete a heuristic inspection using Nielsen's
   usability heuristics plus clinical-safety and accessibility checks.
2. Resolve findings by severity: 0 cosmetic, 1 minor, 2 major, 3 critical.
3. Run role-specific tasks with representative patient, doctor, nurse, and admin
   participants after approval.
4. Record task success, assistance, time, errors, confidence, and comments.
5. Administer the ten-item System Usability Scale without changing its wording.
6. Report participant count, role, inclusion criteria, missing responses, median
   task time, completion rate, error rate, SUS distribution, and limitations.

## Required tasks

- Find the latest reading and its timestamp.
- Switch assigned patients and confirm that the patient context changes.
- Interpret a risk result and identify the strongest supporting evidence.
- Locate model version, dataset, freshness, performance, and limitations.
- Acknowledge an alert and find it in notification history.
- Export the appropriate report without exposing another patient's data.

## Stop conditions

Stop and record a critical finding if a participant sees another patient's data,
cannot distinguish emergency guidance, interprets an unavailable model as a
successful prediction, or cannot recover from a destructive action.
